Privacy Policy

Scope and operator

This policy describes Raymond’s personal Google Workspace integration with his Hermes Agent profiles. It is not the privacy policy of Google, Nous Research, or any AI provider.

Information accessed

Depending on the authorized task, the integration can access Gmail messages and metadata; calendar events and settings; Drive files and metadata; Docs content; spreadsheet content; and contact details. Access is limited by the Google permissions granted and the assistant’s configured operating rules.

Use

Google data is used to carry out requested tasks, such as searching, summarizing, drafting, scheduling, editing, and maintaining approved records. The integration is not operated as an advertising or data-sale service.

AI processing and other services

Relevant Google data retrieved by the assistant may be included in prompts or tool results sent to the AI provider configured for that Hermes profile. Provider-specific processing and retention rules apply. When explicitly authorized, data may also be sent to task destinations such as message recipients or approved record systems. Do not assume all data stays on the operator’s computer.

Storage and security

Google authorization credentials are stored in the operator’s local integration environment. Retrieved data and derived outputs may persist in conversation history, logs, downloaded files, and approved workflow records. Credentials and private Workspace data must not be published to the website or its public source repository.

Retention and deletion

No fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Local records may remain until the operator removes them. External AI providers and task destinations have their own retention rules. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase copies already stored locally or sent to an authorized destination.

Control and revocation

The operator can revoke access through the Google Account third-party connections settings and delete local integration credentials and retained records. Sharing, deletion, sending, and other material writes remain subject to the assistant’s approval rules.

Contact and updates

The operator is Raymond, GitHub account Moohpandu. This integration is for the operator’s own accounts and is not offered for public enrollment. These disclosures should be reviewed whenever providers, permissions, storage, or task destinations change.
