Hermes Agent

Raymond’s personal Google Workspace integration for his Hermes Agent profiles.

Purpose

This integration helps the operator use an AI assistant to find and summarize information and carry out explicitly authorized productivity tasks. It is for personal use, not a public service.

Google services

  • Gmail: read, compose, send, label, and manage messages.
  • Calendar: read and manage calendars and events.
  • Drive: find, read, create, edit, and manage files.
  • Docs and Sheets: read, create, and edit documents and spreadsheets.
  • Contacts: read and manage contact records.

Google authorization grants technical capabilities. It does not replace the operator’s task-specific approval requirements. Some granted scopes also permit deletion and sharing.

Data handling

Data used in an assistant task may be processed by the configured AI provider and retained in local conversation or workflow records. Review the privacy policy and terms before authorizing access.
